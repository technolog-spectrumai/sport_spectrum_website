The value

Your staff should be coaching, not tagging video.

Every club records its matches. Few have the hours to watch them properly. Analysts spend nights clipping footage, insight arrives when the next opponent is already on the bus, and data sits in spreadsheets nobody opens. Sport Spectrum gives that time back and turns it into an edge.

01

Prepare faster

Opponent reports, clip playlists and match summaries arrive early in the week, so there is time to train for what you found.

02

Win the small moments

See which rotations, set plays and matchups quietly win or lose points. These are the details that decide close games.

03

Protect your players

Spot rising load and fatigue patterns early, so medical and coaching staff can adjust before a niggle becomes an absence.

04

Decide with evidence

Back selection, contracts and recruitment with objective, comparable data, not only the last game everyone remembers.

Questions

What clubs usually ask first.

Do we need special cameras or wearables?

Not to get started. We begin with the match and training video you already record. If extra cameras or data sources would help answer your questions, we will explain why before you invest in anything.

Will this replace our analysts?

No. It removes the repetitive work of manual tagging, clipping and spreadsheet building, so your analysts can spend their time on insight, communication and preparation.

Can non-technical staff use it?

Yes. Insights arrive as clear reports, clip playlists and plain-language summaries, and every number opens the video moment behind it.

How do we know it works for us?

The first pilot is built around one question and your own recent matches, so you judge the results on games you know inside out.

Which sports do you cover?

We are developing the engine for team sports, starting with football (soccer) and volleyball. We plan to expand to other sports. Get in touch and tell us about yours.

Is it only for professional clubs?

No. Automation is what makes elite-style analysis affordable for academies, semi-professional clubs and federations without large analyst departments.
